    Power (MW) is how much the grid has to deliver at once. Energy (GWh) is how much it delivers over time. 1 MW running all year is 8.76 GWh.

    Percent

    Hardware cost counts the GPU systems only. Networking and storage add power, which the calculator includes, but they have no published price here, so treat the total as a floor. Facility cost uses the midpoint of a published per-megawatt range. Energy and electricity assume full load all year, which is an upper bound. GB200 NVL72 pricing is an analyst estimate, not a list price. Electricity rates are EIA average industrial prices for November 2025 (Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A). Sources for every figure are listed in the README. This is a planning calculator, not a forecast.
