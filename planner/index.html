    Convert power to yearly energy

    Power (MW) is how much the grid has to deliver at once. Energy (GWh) is how much it delivers over time. 1 MW running all year is 8.76 GWh.

    Percent

    Hardware cost counts the GPU systems only. Networking and storage add power, which the calculator includes, but they have no published price here, so treat the total as a floor. Facility cost uses the midpoint of a published per-megawatt range. Energy and electricity assume full load all year, which is an upper bound. GB200 NVL72 pricing is an analyst estimate, not a list price. Electricity rates are EIA average industrial prices for November 2025 (Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A). This is a planning calculator, not a forecast.

    Sources

    Every number the calculator uses comes from one of these. The notebook has the complete list, with each value next to its source.

    Training compute and model size
    Kaplan et al. (2020), Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models (compute ≈ 6 × parameters × tokens)
    Hoffmann et al. (2022), Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models (about 20 tokens per parameter)
    Speed efficiency, training time, and the Llama comparison
    Meta, The Llama 3 Herd of Models (3.8×10²⁵ FLOP, 38–43% MFU, over 90% effective training time)
    GPU speed, rack power, and price
    NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 and DGX GB200 user guide (about 120 kW per rack)
    NVIDIA DGX H100 user guide (10.2 kW per system)
    Tom's Hardware (analyst estimate of about $3M per GB200 NVL72 rack, not a list price)
    Networking and storage power
    Vertiv 360AI Reference Design 020 (6,912 kW total IT load for a GB200 NVL72 build)
    Facility overhead and build cost
    Uptime Institute Global Data Center Survey 2025 (average PUE of 1.54)
    Cushman & Wakefield 2026 Data Center Development Cost Guide ($8.9M to $23.3M per MW)
    Electricity rates
    U.S. EIA, Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A (average industrial price by state, November 2025)
